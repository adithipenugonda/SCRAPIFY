## 📌 Problem Statement

Traditional scrap collection and recycling systems are inefficient, unorganized, and lack transparency.

### Current Challenges

- ❌ Households struggle to find reliable scrap collectors.
- ❌ Scrap pickup scheduling is mostly manual and time-consuming.
- ❌ Users have no way to track pickup status in real time.
- ❌ Recycling awareness and user engagement are low.
- ❌ Scrap collectors lack optimized job management systems.
- ❌ Waste that could be recycled often ends up in landfills.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

**Scrapify** is an AI-powered waste recycling and scrap collection platform that connects users, collectors, and administrators through a single digital ecosystem.

The platform enables:

- ♻️ Easy Scrap Pickup Scheduling
- 📍 Real-Time Pickup Tracking
- 🚚 Collector Management
- 🎁 Reward-Based Recycling Incentives
- 💳 Digital Payment Support
- 📊 Admin Monitoring & Analytics

### 🌱 Core Vision

Transform waste management into a **smart, transparent, and rewarding experience** while promoting sustainable recycling practices.
